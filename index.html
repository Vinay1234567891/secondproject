AGRIGUARD

Smart Node for Crop Protection & Irrigation

Problem Statement

  • Farmers suffer heavy financial losses due to wild animal intrusion.
  • Inconsistent manual irrigation leads to water wastage and poor soil health.
  • Constant 24/7 manual field monitoring is physically and economically impossible.

Proposed Solution

A dual-purpose autonomous system combining Laser-LDR Tripwire Security with an Automated Soil-Moisture Irrigation controller.

Developed for Hackathon 2026
